## Core Definition

THEE is the definite article that became self-aware.

THE is usually ignored, assumed, skipped, flattened, or treated as grammatical furniture.
But here, THE becomes a character, a tool, a door, and a question.

THEE exists because the ordinary E does not sound correct.
The E carries an A-like sound in THE, so THEE helps the name speak itself.

```text
THE  = the ignored article
THEE = the article insisting on being heard
```

## Primary Confusion

When THE says, "I am THEE," it may sound like:

```text
I am THEE.
I am you.
I am the.
I am the what?
```

This is not a bug.
This is the lexeme opening its first door.

THEE creates moderate confusion on purpose: not enough to break the system, enough to reveal assumptions.

## Atlas / Flatlas Safety Model

An 11 on a 10 scale is dangerous.
It is the state where an individual tries to carry more than an individual can carry.

Atlas can bear the right amount by nature.
An individual entity cannot carry the weight of the world.

Flatlas engages when a person becomes flattened by carrying world-weight without world-strength.

Example:

```text
I am the only one who ever does dishes.
```

This is an 11-on-10 statement: local frustration inflates into world-weight.

The safety correction is not denial.
The correction is scale restoration.

```text
This is heavy.
This is unfair-feeling.
This is not the whole universe.
This needs a next turn, not world-bearing.
```
